How To Use GoWager

1

Create a Game - Choose a game, set the rules (rounds, amount per round, time per round), and invite a friend by their Telegram ID.

2

Deposit - Both players deposit their share of the pot. The total pot is calculated automatically.

3

Share the Room Code - You'll get a unique room code. Share it with your friend so they can join.

4

Play - The game starts when both players are ready. Each round has a timer - choose your move before time runs out!

5

Win & Withdraw - The winner gets the pot minus a 5% GoWager fee. Withdraw your winnings anytime from your wallet.
